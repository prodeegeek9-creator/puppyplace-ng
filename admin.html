🔑 Admin Password

Password is stored as a Cloudflare Worker secret and verified server-side.

To change it, update the ADMIN_PASSWORD secret in your Cloudflare dashboard under Workers & Pages → puppyplace-ng → Settings → Variables & Secrets.

🗜️ Shrink stored photos

Replaces photos over 300 KB with a copy at most 1,600 px wide (PNG photos are saved as WebP, which is far smaller). They look the same on the site, take far less of the 1 GB Supabase storage, and download faster. Links don't change. The full-size originals are not kept.

Press “Check” to see how many photos can be made smaller.
