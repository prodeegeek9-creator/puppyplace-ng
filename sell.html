Sell on PuppyPlace

Reach pet lovers across Nigeria — breeders, pet owners rehoming a pet, and pet product brands.

Who can sell

  • Breeders and pet owners — list puppies, kittens and other pets for sale or adoption on our Pets page.
  • Brands and distributors — stock your pet food, accessories or health products in our shop.

How it works

  1. Send us a message on WhatsApp with what you'd like to list.
  2. We check the details. For pets, that means clear photos, the breed, age, location and vaccination status.
  3. We publish your listing. Buyers contact you directly on WhatsApp.

Our standards

To keep buyers and animals safe, we only list healthy animals that are old enough to leave their mother, with honest descriptions and real photos. We do not list endangered or protected species. We can remove any listing that breaks these rules or our Terms & Conditions.
