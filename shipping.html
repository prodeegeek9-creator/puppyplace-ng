Shipping Information

Where we deliver, how long it takes and how much it costs.

Where we deliver

We deliver pet food, accessories and supplies to addresses across Nigeria.

Delivery times

  • Lagos (mainland & island): 1–2 business days.
  • Other states: 2–5 business days, depending on courier availability.
  • Remote areas: up to 7 business days. We will contact you if we expect a delay.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Delivery estimates start from when your order is confirmed and are not guaranteed.

Delivery fees

Fees depend on your location and the size of your order. The exact fee is shown at checkout, before you pay.

Failed deliveries

If nobody is available to receive your order, we try again the next business day. After two failed attempts the order may be returned to us and a re-delivery fee charged. Please double-check your address and phone number at checkout.

Tracking your order

See Track Order.

Live animals
