Who We Are

Built for Nigerian Pet Owners

Looking after a pet in Nigeria can be harder than it should be. Good food, grooming products and accessories are not always easy to find nearby, and it can be difficult to know who to trust when looking for a new pet.

PuppyPlace.ng was created to help. We bring pet food, treats, toys, grooming supplies, health products and accessories together in one online store, with secure payment and delivery to your door. Alongside the shop, our pets page lists dogs, cats and other animals for sale or adoption, and our blog shares practical advice on feeding, health, grooming and training.

Our Mission

Making Pet Care Easier

We want every pet in Nigeria to be healthy, happy and well looked after. Our job is to make that easier for their owners: good products at fair prices, clear information, and friendly help when you need it.
