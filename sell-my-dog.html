What you'll need

Have these ready and your listing will be live sooner.

  • The breed (or "mixed breed")
  • Age, and the sex of each puppy
  • Your asking price, or "adoption" if rehoming
  • Your city and area, e.g. Lekki, Lagos
  • Vaccination and deworming status
  • Pedigree papers, if you have them
  • 2–6 clear, recent photos in daylight
  • The WhatsApp number buyers should message

Tips to sell your dog faster

📸 Take good photos

Shoot in daylight at the dog's eye level, with a clean background. Include one full-body photo and one close-up of the face. For a litter, photograph each puppy separately.

💰 Price it fairly

Check similar dogs on our pets page before you set a price. Age, pedigree, vaccinations and the parents' quality all affect what buyers will pay.

💉 Show health records

Buyers pay more for vaccinated, dewormed dogs. Mention the vaccines given and have the vaccination card ready to show.

⚡ Reply quickly

Most buyers message several sellers. Answering within the hour, with extra photos or a short video, wins the sale.

Our listing standards

To keep buyers and animals safe, we only publish healthy dogs that are at least 8 weeks old, weaned and eating on their own, with real, recent photos and honest descriptions. We don't list stolen, sick or protected animals. We can decline or remove any listing that breaks these rules or our Terms & Conditions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sell my dog in Nigeria?
Tap List my dog free to chat with our listing assistant on WhatsApp. Send the breed, age, price, location and a few clear photos. We check the details and publish your listing on the PuppyPlace pets page, and buyers contact you directly on WhatsApp.
Is it free to list my dog on PuppyPlace?
Yes. Listing a dog or puppies for sale or adoption on PuppyPlace.ng is free.
Where can I sell puppies in Lagos or Abuja?
PuppyPlace.ng lists dogs from sellers across Nigeria, including Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Benin City and Enugu. Add your city when you list so nearby buyers can find you.
How long before my listing goes live?
Every listing is checked by the PuppyPlace team before it is published. We look at the photos and details, and contact you on WhatsApp if anything is missing.
How do buyers pay for the dog?
Buyers contact you directly on WhatsApp and you agree the sale between you. We advise buyers to see the dog and its health records before paying.
How old must puppies be before I sell them?
Puppies should be at least 8 weeks old, weaned and eating on their own before they leave their mother. We do not list younger puppies.
Can I sell a dog without papers?
Yes. Many dogs in Nigeria are sold without pedigree papers. Just list the dog as non-pedigree and describe it honestly.
Can I rehome my dog for free instead of selling?
Yes. Tell the assistant you want to list your dog for adoption and leave out the price. Your listing appears as an adoption listing.
